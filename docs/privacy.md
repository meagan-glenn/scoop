# Scoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Scoop is a pet health tracker made by Meagan Glenn. It is built to keep your household's records on your own devices.

## What Scoop stores

Everything you log in Scoop (animals, episodes, photos, notes, exposures) is stored on your device. There are no Scoop servers and no Scoop account.

## Household sync (optional)

If you turn on household sync, your records are stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, and shared only with the people you invite. Meagan Glenn has no access to that data. Apple's iCloud terms apply.

## AI features (optional)

Scoop has two AI features, both powered by Anthropic's API and both off unless the build includes an API key.

- **Photo scoring.** If you attach a photo to an entry, that photo is sent to Anthropic so it can be scored. Photos are taken with the camera inside Scoop and are never saved to your photo library.
- **Vet summary opening line.** When you open a pet's vet summary, the summary's own bullet points (your pet's name, age, breed, and any conditions you entered, plus medication names and doses, dates, and stool counts) are sent to Anthropic so it can phrase a short opening statement. Your name and your note to the vet are not sent.

In both cases nothing else is sent: no account, location, or device identifiers. Anthropic's API usage policies apply. Neither feature is used for advertising. If the build has no API key, no data ever leaves your device.

## What Scoop does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond the optional services above (iCloud sync and the AI features)
- No account, no email collection

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all local data. Turn off household sync (or delete the household from iCloud settings) to remove synced data.
